<Callout>
    The intent of Easter Egg Dimensions is for them to be found naturally during play. This list is provided for your curiosity and reference material, but goes against the intent of the mod's exploration mechanics.
</Callout>

Like the snapshot that inspired the mod, Infinite Dimensions contains its own list of Easter Egg dimensions. Some of the ones present in the original mod were brought back, and others were created for the mod itself. This list serves as a reference for your exploration.

## MISSINGNO
![A dimension composed of endless sepia hallways, illuminated by yellow lights in the ceiling.](https://mirror.tangled.network/xrpc/sh.tangled.git.temp.getBlob?path=assets%2Fbackrooms.avif&ref=master&repo=did%3Aplc%3Aa4keyp4vazq32au7zzxrq4pc)
- Key: [missingno/no key provided]

When an empty book is given to an Infinity Portal, it creates a missing dimension. Featureless and completely barren, the tunnels of this empty world go on for eternity. Be careful not to get lost.

## Blue
![A forest dimension tinted with a blue filter.](https://mirror.tangled.network/xrpc/sh.tangled.git.temp.getBlob?path=assets%2Fblue.avif&ref=master&repo=did%3Aplc%3Aa4keyp4vazq32au7zzxrq4pc)
- Key: blue

This dimension may resemble the Overworld in its terrain, but its vibrant blue tint makes it hard to make out.

## Chaos
![A dimension composed of randomly generated terrain. Each block is entirely random.](https://mirror.tangled.network/xrpc/sh.tangled.git.temp.getBlob?path=assets%2Fchaos.avif&ref=master&repo=did%3Aplc%3Aa4keyp4vazq32au7zzxrq4pc)
- Key: chaos

The Chaos dimension is perhaps the most stark reminder of the multiverse's penchant for randomness. The terrain is generated from completely random blocks, accented by a harsh green sky.

## Classic
![A dimension with vibrant green grass and a large brick pyramid.](https://mirror.tangled.network/xrpc/sh.tangled.git.temp.getBlob?path=assets%2Fclassic.avif&ref=master&repo=did%3Aplc%3Aa4keyp4vazq32au7zzxrq4pc)
- Key: classic, origin

The Classic dimension is a reminder of a world long since left behind. Adventurers may note its classic structures and nostalgic terrain.

## Chess
![A dimension with large black and white wool tiles and trees.](https://mirror.tangled.network/xrpc/sh.tangled.git.temp.getBlob?path=assets%2Fcheckerboard.avif&ref=master&repo=did%3Aplc%3Aa4keyp4vazq32au7zzxrq4pc)
- Key: chess

This chess board is perhaps the opposite of the Chaos dimension. Peak uniformity, a perfect pattern of black and white squares, accented by black and white trees. The denizens of this dimension are quite violent, if provoked.

## Colors
![An aerial view of a dimension composed of brightly colored wool and concrete, as well as lakes of a vibrant colored iridescent fluid.](https://mirror.tangled.network/xrpc/sh.tangled.git.temp.getBlob?path=assets%2Fcolors.avif&ref=master&repo=did%3Aplc%3Aa4keyp4vazq32au7zzxrq4pc)
- Key: colors

A bright dimension with a sky shifting through hues, full of colourful blocks. The liquid that makes up the lakes, known as iridescence, causes a strange reaction when touched.

## Content
![A skyblock dimension with a single Oak Tree and a chest.](https://mirror.tangled.network/xrpc/sh.tangled.git.temp.getBlob?path=assets%2Fcontent.avif&ref=master&repo=did%3Aplc%3Aa4keyp4vazq32au7zzxrq4pc)
- Key: content

The Content dimension is comprised of single, familiarly shaped, island in the void, stocked with only a chest of rare resources and a single Oak Tree. A brave explorer might be tempted to live out their life in the dimension, using its meager resources to create something beautiful in the void.

## Credits
![A void dimension with a dark purple sky with credits text.](https://mirror.tangled.network/xrpc/sh.tangled.git.temp.getBlob?path=assets%2Fcredits.avif&ref=master&repo=did%3Aplc%3Aa4keyp4vazq32au7zzxrq4pc)
- Key: credits

The Credits dimension pays homage to those who made this experience possible.

## Debug
![A void dimension composed of a flat grid of blocks.](https://mirror.tangled.network/xrpc/sh.tangled.git.temp.getBlob?path=assets%2Fdebug.avif&ref=master&repo=did%3Aplc%3Aa4keyp4vazq32au7zzxrq4pc)
- Key: debug

The Debug dimension generates an impossible grid of every block and every state in the game.

## Green
![A forest dimension tinted with a green filter.](https://mirror.tangled.network/xrpc/sh.tangled.git.temp.getBlob?path=assets%2Fgreen.avif&ref=master&repo=did%3Aplc%3Aa4keyp4vazq32au7zzxrq4pc)
- Key: green

This dimension may resemble the Overworld in its terrain, but its vibrant green tint makes it hard to make out.

## Hills
![2024-05-04_17 22 38](https://mirror.tangled.network/xrpc/sh.tangled.git.temp.getBlob?path=assets%2Fhills.avif&ref=master&repo=did%3Aplc%3Aa4keyp4vazq32au7zzxrq4pc)
- Key: hills

The lovely world of the Hills is a peaceful one, reminiscent of the Overworld but for the hills that give the dimension its name. As each hill is bordered by a large lake, each individual hill seems taller than they would otherwise.

## Library
![A dimension composed of vibrant rainbox bookshelves as far as the eye can see.](https://mirror.tangled.network/xrpc/sh.tangled.git.temp.getBlob?path=assets%2Flibrary.avif&ref=master&repo=did%3Aplc%3Aa4keyp4vazq32au7zzxrq4pc)
- Key: library

Composed of Boxes of Infinite Books, the Infinite Library is an endlessly repeating dimension. It can become easy to lose one's way, left to wander its infinite halls. The Box of Infinite Books, exclusive to the dimension, generates a random book when right clicked.

## Nexus
![A beta-esque tunnel stretching to infinity with colored portals lining it on both sides](https://mirror.tangled.network/xrpc/sh.tangled.git.temp.getBlob?path=assets%2Fnexus.avif&ref=master&repo=did%3Aplc%3Aa4keyp4vazq32au7zzxrq4pc)
- Key: nexus

Some may call the Nexus the center of the multiverse. At this intersection point, you can find Infinity Portals to endless numbers of other dimensions. The dimension's telltale simple construction speaks to its construction taking place in a time long ago.

## Pride
![A vibrant paradise dimension with a colourful rainbow stripe. The water of a lake is dyed pink.](https://mirror.tangled.network/xrpc/sh.tangled.git.temp.getBlob?path=assets%2Fpride.avif&ref=master&repo=did%3Aplc%3Aa4keyp4vazq32au7zzxrq4pc)
- Key: pride

The Pride dimension is a cutesy paradise. Explorers will note the literal "rainbow road" of dyed wool, the pink sky and vibrant natural life.

## Random Forest
![A forest dimension composed of different types of trees and tall mushrooms.](https://mirror.tangled.network/xrpc/sh.tangled.git.temp.getBlob?path=assets%2Frandom_forest.avif&ref=master&repo=did%3Aplc%3Aa4keyp4vazq32au7zzxrq4pc)

![A forest dimension tinted with a red filter.](https://mirror.tangled.network/xrpc/sh.tangled.git.temp.getBlob?path=assets%2Fred.avif&ref=master&repo=did%3Aplc%3Aa4keyp4vazq32au7zzxrq4pc)
- Key: red

This dimension may resemble the Overworld in its terrain, but its vibrant red tint makes it hard to make out.

## Skygrid
![A void dimension with random blocks arranged in a three dimensional grid pattern.](https://mirror.tangled.network/xrpc/sh.tangled.git.temp.getBlob?path=assets%2Fskygrid.avif&ref=master&repo=did%3Aplc%3Aa4keyp4vazq32au7zzxrq4pc)
- Key: skygrid

The Skygrid dimension is another one of strange uniformity, hearkening back to old worlds and old challenges.

## Slime
![A dimension with bright green grass and Slimes with a variety of tints.](https://mirror.tangled.network/xrpc/sh.tangled.git.temp.getBlob?path=assets%2Fslime.avif&ref=master&repo=did%3Aplc%3Aa4keyp4vazq32au7zzxrq4pc)
- Key: slime

The Slime dimension is named after its denizens, multicoloured Chaos Slimes with random items stored inside. The dimension's vibrant green grass and cyan skies may make it an appealing one to travel to, but the Chaos Slimes guard their territory - and the items inside of it - quite protectively, and will not be easily parted with either.

## Spiral
![A dimension with a spiral land pattern, with Cherry trees.](https://mirror.tangled.network/xrpc/sh.tangled.git.temp.getBlob?path=assets%2Fspiral.avif&ref=master&repo=did%3Aplc%3Aa4keyp4vazq32au7zzxrq4pc)
- Key: spiral

The odd uniformity of the Spiral makes it a sight to behold from above, though the further you go from the dimension's origin point the less it resembles a spiral, and more just a circle. The lovely Cherry Grove that makes up the landmass of this dimension's landmass may make it an appealing one for travelers after the pink wood.

## Sponge
![A dimension arranged in the pattern of a Menger Sponge.](https://mirror.tangled.network/xrpc/sh.tangled.git.temp.getBlob?path=assets%2Fsponge.avif&ref=master&repo=did%3Aplc%3Aa4keyp4vazq32au7zzxrq4pc)
- Key: sponge

What could have prompted the strange uniformity of the Sponge Dimension is unknowable. Its stark, unnatural appearance and orange-tinted sky make it a sight to behold.
